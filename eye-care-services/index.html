Eye Care Services in New Braunfels

Welcome to Eye Care New Braunfels, where comprehensive vision care meets urgent eye health needs. Our practice is committed to offering a full spectrum of eye care services tailored to accommodate every aspect of your vision and eye health. From routine eye exams and personalized eye condition care to the latest in disease management, we are here to ensure your eyesight is at its best.

We also provide emergency eye care services, ensuring that immediate attention is available for those unexpected eye health issues. Trust us to be your all-encompassing partner in maintaining optimal eye health and vision clarity.

Thorough Eye Exams at Eye Care New Braunfels

Our eye exams go beyond simply determining your prescription. They are a critical part of maintaining overall eye health, designed to detect early signs of eye conditions and other health issues. Utilizing the latest in eye care technology, our experienced team conducts thorough examinations, assessing your vision and the health of your eyes inside and out.

Whether you’re experiencing vision changes, need an update to your glasses or contact lens prescription, or it’s just time for your regular check-up, our comprehensive eye exams are tailored to meet your individual needs. Prioritize your vision and eye health with our expert care.

Meeting Your Eye Health Needs with Current Technology

At our clinic, we take pride in utilizing the latest advancements in eye care technology to improve the accuracy of diagnoses and the effectiveness of treatments. Our state-of-the-art equipment enables us to identify eye conditions at their earliest stages, resulting in precise prescriptions and customized treatment plans.

From digital retinal imaging to advanced corneal mapping, our technology suite supports a wide range of services. This dedication to cutting-edge technology forms the foundation of our commitment to offering you the highest standard of eye care, guaranteeing the protection and preservation of your vision with the utmost precision.

Our Technology

  • Humphrey Matrix FDT Visual Field

    An ideal Glaucoma screening device that provides a clinically verified, fast and affordable means of detecting early visual field loss.

  • Optomap

    The optomap® Retinal Exam produces an image that is as unique as you fingerprint and provides us with a wide view to look at the health of your retina. The scan will show us a healthy eye or detect disease.
