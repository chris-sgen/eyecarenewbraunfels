# eyecarenewbraunfels

> **Unofficial copy — not the website of Eye Care New Braunfels.** This repository is a development copy of
> a redesign, published so that the redesign can be looked at in a browser. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by
> Eye Care New Braunfels. The practice's real site is https://www.eyecarenewbraunfels.com/.
>
> **A snapshot, with its forms switched off.** The pages were read from the practice's site on the evening of October 27, 2026, Central Time (October 27, between 23:30 and 23:31 UTC), and are
> not kept current. The practice's two forms, an appointment request and a patient registration, are shown but disabled: nothing entered in them is sent anywhere.

**Preview: https://chris-sgen.github.io/eyecarenewbraunfels/**

A **redesign** of **eyecarenewbraunfels.com** (Eye Care New Braunfels, an eye care practice in New Braunfels, Texas): 22 pages, made by a rebuild pipeline and changed for public hosting as listed under *Hardening*: the 20 pages of the live site that the crawl found, at their own addresses (among them its own not-found page, `/404-page-not-found/`, and its sitemap page), and a search page and a not-found page that the build makes itself. The live site has no blog: its three category pages list nothing. It is a static site with no backend. To look at it, use the preview link. To serve the folder yourself, use a server that answers a folder's address (`/insurance/`) with the `index.html` inside it, under the path `/eyecarenewbraunfels/` (the not-found page names its files there).

> **1 still picture and 1 silent video loop in this copy are AI-generated illustrations made for this redesign.**
> They are not photographs of the practice, its office, its doctors or its staff, and none shows a person (see *Generated imagery*).
> **The live site publishes no portraits of the practice's two optometrists**: where they would stand, its pages show one placeholder of its web platform, a white figure on a grey square, which this copy, like the private build, replaces with each doctor's initials (Hardening 6). The photograph that appears to show the two doctors at the practice's sign, the photograph of its reception desk and the photograph at the head of the home page are shown where the live site shows them (the first on the home page too).

Two terms used below. The **private build** is the build of this redesign that this copy was made from,
with its documentation and audit files; it is not published. This **preview** is that build with the
changes listed under *Hardening*.

## Hardening applied to this public copy

`PROVENANCE.json` gives the counts.

1. **`noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet, noimageindex` on every page.** This tag is the only index control this copy has. It is a request, not a lock: the large search engines say they follow `noindex`; `noimageindex` is a rule of Google's. A `robots.txt` with `Disallow: /` is also shipped, but it has **no effect** here: crawlers read `robots.txt` only at the host root (`chris-sgen.github.io/robots.txt`, which only a separate repository for that host could provide), not under `/eyecarenewbraunfels/`. GitHub Pages cannot send an `X-Robots-Tag` header either. That leaves every file that is not a page (the pictures, the loop, the fonts, the stylesheets, the script, the search index, the licence text and these documents) with no index control of its own; on this site they are linked only from pages marked `nofollow`. The repository itself is public: github.com lists every file of it, and its page there can be indexed.
2. **Link-preview tags.** `og:url` points at this preview, `og:description` / `twitter:description` carry the disclosure (`twitter:description` is added on the 9 pages of the private build that had none), `og:site_name` reads "Unofficial preview (not Eye Care New Braunfels)", and `og:title` / `twitter:title` begin "Unofficial preview:" (22 pages), with the practice's name taken off where it stands at the end of the title after a bar or a dash ("… | Eye Care New Braunfels"); a title that names the practice inside its own words keeps them. The picture tags (`og:image`, `twitter:image`, `og:featured_image`: 46 tags) are removed and `twitter:card` is `summary`, the small card, so no tag offers a picture. The practice's icon is not this copy's tab or home-screen icon (66 icon links removed); an empty icon stands in. **The `<title>` of a page is not marked**: it is the private build's title, the live page's own (C06 above names the three that are made from a heading). What a link card shows depends on the app: apps that take a link's description or site name from these tags show the disclosure; apps that read none of these tags show the page's own title with the `chris-sgen.github.io` address; apps that pick a picture from the page itself may show one of its pictures.
3. **JSON-LD removed** (204 blocks). It asserted, as data for search engines, the practice's identity, address and telephone, and the trail and kind of each page.
4. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` on every page, so that, in browsers that honour the tag, a click on an outbound link does not tell its target this address. The 21 map frames carry `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` themselves, because a policy on a frame overrules the page's. No page holds a film: the live site has none. The script's code for a film frame (unused here; it would load `youtube-nocookie.com`) would send only this site's bare origin.
5. **The practice's two forms are switched off.** The appointment request form (`/contact-us/appointment-request-form/`) and the patient registration form (`/contact-us/patient-registration-form/`) are shown as the private build draws them from the live forms, but each is inert three ways over: it is a dialog form with an empty address that cancels its own sending (`method="dialog"`, `action=""`, `onsubmit="return false"`), a hidden disabled send button and a hidden disabled field stand first in it, so that pressing Enter in a field sends nothing, and its own button is disabled. A notice in the head of each page says that this preview is not the practice's website, that the form on the page is disabled and that nothing entered in it is sent or saved, and gives the practice's telephone number; a shorter line right above each form, and the status line under it, say that the form is disabled and nothing is sent. The private build's own lines in each form, which say, at its top and under its button, that online sending is not connected yet and ask the visitor to call, are taken out, with the template its script fills. The 26 parts of the patient registration form that its rules show only after an answer (boxes for an "other" answer, questions that follow from an earlier answer, the details of a second insurance) are hidden before any answer, as the private build's script hides them when the page loads: that script does not run for an inert form, so this copy marks them hidden itself (26 of 26), and they stay hidden whatever is answered. The trials under *Verification* show the forms inert, with scripts on and off. Every "Request Appointment" button (136, on every page) leads, as the live site's booking buttons do, to `/contact-us/appointment-request-form/`, so nothing can be booked online from this copy. **The site search works**: its 27 forms send the words only to this copy's own `/search/` page, as part of that page's address.
6. **The doctors are shown by their initials; no picture the private build shows is left out.** The live site publishes no portrait of either of the practice's two optometrists: on `/`, `/our-optometric-team/`, `/team/dr-kevin-reynolds-od/` and `/team/dr-noemi-lozano-od/`, where a portrait would stand, it shows one placeholder of its web platform, a white figure on a grey square, the same for both doctors (4 file addresses, 2 pictures by their bytes, one per size), described by each doctor's name ("Dr. Kevin Reynolds, O.D." and "Dr. Noemi Lozano, O.D."). The private build draws each doctor's initials instead ("KR" and "NL") in the 6 places where the live pages show the placeholder, and on two cards it adds, one at the foot of each doctor's own page, which lead to the other doctor's page (8 in all, on 4 pages); it prints each name as text beside the initials, and this copy keeps all of it. **The photograph that appears to show the practice's two doctors before its sign is shown**, where the live team page shows it, and the redesign also shows it on the home page beside the doctors; **the photograph of the practice's reception desk and the photograph at the head of the home page** (a family on a picnic, in sunglasses) are shown as on the live home page. This copy leaves out none of the pictures the private build shows on its pages, and every distinct picture of this copy was looked at before publishing, on contact sheets. The other photographs of people in this copy illustrate the topics of the pages (eye examinations, contact lenses, eyeglasses, sunglasses and the like, and the picture of the not-found pages); by their look (most also by their file names) they are stock photographs posed by models (none was traced to its stock library).
7. **No on-page disclosure banner.** The disclosure is carried by the link-preview tags (in apps that show them; see 2) and by this README. Apart from the notes named here a page looks like a page of the practice: its name, its address, telephone number and hours, and its words. 6 pages carry a note of this preview in their head, under their title: the two form pages (Hardening 5), and four pages whose own words speak about the live website or the practice's services, or invite visitors to get in touch (`/disclaimer/`, `/privacy-policy/`, `/website-accessibility-policy/`, `/contact-us/`), whose note says that the words on the page are the live site's own and speak for the practice and its live website, not for this preview, which is a separate copy that is not the practice's. The notes speak of "this preview" or "this copy"; none says "unofficial".

## Known limits

- **No form of the practice can be sent and nothing can be booked online from this copy.** See Hardening 5. The site search's forms send words only to this copy's `/search/` page; 25 mail links (one in the footer of every page, and one more on each of `/contact-us/`, `/location/eye-care-new-braunfels/` and `/website-accessibility-policy/`) open a message to the address the live pages give (see *Links that lead to live services*). This copy's pages and script set no cookie and keep nothing in the browser (measured locally, with the map frames blocked); the Google map frames are Google's, and what they do is Google's. The host, GitHub Pages, receives every request as any web host does and keeps its own logs.
- **This is a snapshot.** The pages were read on the evening of October 27, 2026, Central Time (October 27, between 23:30 and 23:31 UTC); their pictures and stylesheets were downloaded by 23:38 UTC, and the live pages were loaded in a browser, from 23:40 to 23:47 UTC on October 27 (the end at 6:47 PM on October 27, Central Time), to measure their look and to see which words their scripts add. Opening hours, insurance plans, brands and services stand as the live site showed them then. **The top bar gives the day's hours from the snapshot (after the word "Today" on wider screens), and the hours tables mark that day**: the day is the one of the visitor's own clock, not Central Time, and on a holiday or after a change of hours the line is wrong. The practice's own site is the place for what holds today.
- **The words on the pages are the live site's, and some of them speak about the live site or invite a visitor to use it** ("Content on this web site", on `/disclaimer/`; "Accessibility features of this website", on `/website-accessibility-policy/`; "We invite you to get in touch with us", on `/contact-us/`). The pages named in Hardening 7 carry a note; elsewhere the practice's words are carried as written, and so are the descriptions of its pictures.
- **4 of the 20 live pages carried here ask search engines not to index them** (`noindex`: its not-found page and its three category pages); here every page asks it.
- **The generated pictures and the loop are illustrations, not the practice's photographs.** No caption or frame on the pages tells them apart; `PROVENANCE.md` lists each one.
- **The video loop has no pause control.** It is silent and hidden from screen readers, it plays only on or near the screen, and when the device asks for reduced motion or data saving it does not play at all and its first frame shows instead. A pause made through the browser's or the system's media controls is undone while the loop is on the screen. WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) asks for a way to pause moving content that starts by itself and lasts more than five seconds; this design does not provide one.
- **People.** The names, credentials and biographies of the practice's two optometrists are carried as the live site shows them; the live site shows no photograph of either on their own (their initials stand where the live pages show the platform's placeholder, Hardening 6), and the photograph that appears to show both at the practice's sign is shown where the live team page shows it and on the home page. The one review of the live home page is carried with its reviewer's name as the live page prints it; its words name a member of the practice's staff by her first name.
- **Links that lead to live services.** 144 links dial the practice's telephone number. 25 mail links open a message to `eyecarenb2020@yahoo.com`. The links to the practice's accounts, in the footer of every page as in the live footer: `facebook.com` (22: its Facebook page) and `maps.app.goo.gl` (22: its place on Google Maps). No link of this copy leads to the practice's own site; the notes name its address without a link.
- **Links to other organisations.** `google.com`, 44 links: directions to the practice on Google Maps ("Directions", 43 links, which this redesign adds: the live pages have none) and a Google search for the practice's name and address that opens its reviews there ("Read Our Reviews", 1 link, on the home page, as on the live home page); `get.adobe.com`, 1 link: Adobe's page for its free PDF reader ("Download Adobe Acrobat", on `/website-accessibility-policy/`, as on the live page). The 21 map frames, one on every page but `/search/`, load from Google Maps when they near the screen, and the browser then talks to Google's servers; the frames carry `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` (Hardening 4).
- **Nothing forwards an old or misspelled address** (Hardening 10); such an address shows the not-found page. How the host treats capitals and a missing closing slash had not yet been measured at the public address when this file was written.
- **File names.** A picture's file is named after the name the live site gave it (in small letters, shortened), with a short code added; 24 names are the live site's codes (22 logos of insurers and of eyewear brands, such as `29umjpgjw56kh0x4`, and the two pictures of instruments on the services page, such as `1167f65a-…`), and, apart from the names of brands and of the practice, none keeps a personal name: the files of the platform's placeholder, whose names on the live site begin with each doctor's first name, are not shipped (Hardening 6 and 10). What a page says in its text, in the descriptions of its pictures and in the tags of its head was read for the names of the live site's system; the addresses of its files were not.
- **Tested in Chrome 156 (headless, on Windows) only.** Firefox and Safari were not run. Phone and tablet figures are emulated widths, not devices.
