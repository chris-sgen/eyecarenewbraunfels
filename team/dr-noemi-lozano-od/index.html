Dr. Noemi Lozano, O.D.

Request AppointmentCall 830-837-5310
NL

Dr. Noemi "Mimi" Lozano was born and raised in Corpus Christi, Texas. She attended and enjoyed playing on the lady’s tennis team at the University of Texas at San Antonio. Off the courts, she received her BS degree in Biology. After graduation she worked as an optician and optometry technician, then pursued a career in optometry. She received her Doctor of Optometry degree in 2009 from the University of Houston College of Optometry.

She has worked in many optometric settings in central Texas, but most enjoyed practicing at the Veterans Affairs Eye Clinic in Austin. She then decided to move her family to the beautiful, family-oriented town of New Braunfels and began a private practice. When Dr. Lozano is not seeing patients, she enjoys watching her kids play tennis, taking family vacations and exploring new restaurants.
